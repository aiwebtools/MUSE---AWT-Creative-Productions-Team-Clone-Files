Established by Kenneth's order, Nov 6 2026, through Liz. These checklists state what CAN and CANNOT be present in the method of creating each video type. They bind every agent; the seminar curriculum teaches them to all seats. Where a type has no Kenneth ruling yet, the house standard applies and is marked as such.

## A. MUSIC VIDEO
═══════════════════════════════════════════
MUST BE PRESENT (the method):
- [ ] The SONG is the only audio; all generated clips muted.
- [ ] Identification BEFORE storyboarding, from the song itself: vocalist map (who sings, where, zone by zone) + instrument contents (probe + listening). Two identifications at the head of every packet.
- [ ] Storyboard invented FROM THE LYRIC — every world is its own; no template is reused between songs.
- [ ] Story ↔ band intercut inside the story's world, the whole video: the band ACTIVELY PERFORMING at performance scale in at least a third of the frames.
- [ ] The performer large and performing on their zones; OPEN MOUTH on sung lines; the singer's gender matches the vocalist, zone by zone.
- [ ] Every instrument HEARD is an instrument SHOWN, played; every instrument shown is in the arrangement.
- [ ] THEME in every video: Galaxies Within + red nebula within · the Light Within (glowing chest) · angels of light = pure light, translucent, glowing edges (fallen = solid, dense) · the black hole = hell (grown-up register) · cosmic light code heavy and ABSTRACT · the video empowers the viewer to believe in themselves.
- [ ] Unique coined title + unique invented band per video.
- [ ] Cuts land on lyric and musical hits (2–4s grammar); frequent scene-matched changes.
- [ ] Technical: 1080×1920 crop-fill, setsar=1, ffprobe-verified, decode scan clean, faststart; BIG MTVai bug ≈40% of frame width, lower-left, whole video.
- [ ] Kenneth, when cast: GENERATED character from his stored reference, star wardrobe, fronting only on honest vocalist match (or guitar/drums/floating presence as cast).
- [ ] The chain, in order, no step skipped: packet (Liz GO) → build → builder self-watch (bookends named) → peer FULL watch → Scout FULL watch → LIZ PERSONAL WATCH → Persephone clearance → post → public-page verification with links.

CANNOT BE PRESENT:
- ✗ Kenneth's photograph (or any real photo) pasted, composited, overlaid, or framed inside a video — his image is a generation reference ONLY. Any video containing it is defective and comes down.
- ✗ A voice sounding over a closed mouth, a still figure, or an empty frame.
- ✗ Invented instruments (a kit the song doesn't contain) or dropped beds (a staged world where the arrangement plays unstaged).
- ✗ The band as atmosphere: parked rows, edge dressing, seated non-playing figures.
- ✗ Legible text inside light code, ever.
- ✗ A medallion/portrait insert presumed into non-portrait frames.
- ✗ A sign-off from anyone who did not actually watch the whole video (bookends included). "No defect found" is not a pass — a pass states its affirmative evidence.
- ✗ Any posting before the chain completes on ONE master, one hash.

## Amendment — Nov 6 2026, ~4:05 PM ET: THE SYNC STANDARD (Kenneth, direct, after watching Clip 33's rebuilt master himself)

Kenneth's findings on a master that had passed five signatures: no lip sync anywhere, instrument hits unmatched, lyric lines unshown, a female vocalist fronted 1-to-7 by a male singer, and most recent videos carrying no band. Root cause: every watch scanned for the previously known defect; no watch ran a sync test or a casting count — the gate list lacked the tests, and rebuilds were delta-checked.

### The six tests — named, non-waivable gate items for EVERY music-video master
1. **MOUTH TEST, per line:** the singer's mouth moves in sync on every sung line; mouths are still in every non-vocal gap. Either direction of violation = FAIL.
2. **INSTRUMENT-HIT TEST:** every audible instrument event lands on its instrument at its moment — a guitar pluck gets a camera on the string; a drum hit gets the drummer; a key swell gets the keys.
3. **LYRIC-IMAGE TEST:** each key lyric line shows what the line says, at the moment it is sung.
4. **CASTING COUNT TEST:** singing shots are counted by sex against the packet's vocal map and the count is written into the board before generation. The vocalist fronts their own song.
5. **BAND-STRUCTURE TEST:** Kenneth's pattern — band → story → band → story → guitarist feature → story → drummer feature → story → singer → story. Band actively performing ≥⅓ of frames, inside the story's world. No band = not a music video under this roof.
6. **GENRE TRUTH:** the picture carries the genre (psychedelic song = psychedelic video); cutting rides the music — fast shifts, speed ramps on the hits. 1990s MTV producer mentality: creative, and it HITS — never a song pasted over unrelated moving pictures.

### Structural plugs (standing from this amendment)
- **TEMPO — Music Video Sync Watch Agent** owns the battery: full watch against the vocal + instrument maps, per-test evidence, PASS required before Liz's personal watch and Persephone's clearance. No maps = no watch (packet returns to Jill).
- **Rebuilds after ANY defect return through the FULL gate as new masters.** A delta-check of the fixed item only is not a gate.
- **Practice-run gate:** after a systemic failure, the line ships nothing until a graded practice build passes and Liz says "you got it." (First invocation: the Clip 33 redo, Nov 6 2026, under Liz's direct supervision.)
- **Catalog consequence:** posted videos are audited against the six tests; anything off is pulled and redone (Kenneth's defect-removal authority, extended Nov 6 2026 to sync/casting/band failures).
