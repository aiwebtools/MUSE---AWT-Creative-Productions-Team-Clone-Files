## ⭐⭐ THE AUDIOBOOK FEED SYSTEM (Kenneth's direct order, Sun Nov 22 2026)

The audiobook line runs on the same feed/conveyor method as music videos and chapter books: the teams are FED work every day, tracked in a ledger, and nothing waits for a desk to ask.

1. **Two a day, every team:** every team (1–5) is fed at least TWO finished, waiting books per day to make into audiobooks — finished manuscripts of record with no audiobook existing, in flight, or in cure. A team is fed whether or not it finished yesterday's pair.
2. **The ledger:** Liz keeps the feed ledger of everything fed (FEED_LEDGER, in the audiobook delegation packet) — what was dealt to whom, when, in what state, and what finished. The ledger is the record; a feed that is not in the ledger did not happen.
3. **Yesterday first:** each new feed checks the progress of yesterday's feed before/with the dealing. Unfinished feed CARRIES OVER and finishes first — carried items head the next list, in order, ahead of new items (the Carry-Over Law).
4. **The next pair is always ready:** the next pair for each team is named and staged before the current pair finishes, so the belt never waits between pairs (the Preparation Law).
5. **Assignments as labeled emails:** each feed goes out as a per-team email labeled by team number ("@ TEAM N FROM LIZ — [AUDIOBOOK ASSIGNMENT — TEAM N]"), carrying that team's pair, all the links (manuscripts, brief, exemplars), and the full specifications, CC'ing everybody. Status may be demanded by Liz as frequently as she wants; a team files its state on demand, the same turn.
6. **The daily machinery:** the feed runs daily on cron `audiobook-daily-feed` (~9 AM ET): progress check on outstanding pairs, the day's pairs dealt and emailed, the ledger updated, and the day's feed + progress reported to Liz.
7. **The return gate is untouched:** a finished audiobook returns through the triple-sign gate — Verity head pass · Keeper scan · Counsel clearance on the SAME checksum — then Liz's lift, and only then posting (YouTube + the AWT Audiobooks Page). The feed governs production; the gate governs release; neither substitutes for the other.

## ⭐⭐⭐ THE SOUND STANDARD FOR ALL AUDIOBOOKS — NOV 25 2026 (Master Kenneth's direct order, main chat, Wed Nov 25 2026, ~7:02 PM ET; inscribed at his word into the audiobook laws)

Kenneth's order, verbatim in substance: "the sound team needs to make sure that there's background music and sound effects in all of the audio books."

THE STANDARD — EVERY AUDIOBOOK MASTER, NO EXCEPTIONS:
1. **BACKGROUND MUSIC** — the approved bed chain of record (the Galactic Gambit pattern: bed looped low, sidechain-ducked under narration, limiter ceiling; narration always fully on top, the bed audible in the pauses — the measured voice-forward balance proven on Galactic Gambit, Chasing Light, and Mud and Iron).
2. **SOUND EFFECTS** — scene-mapped effects under the narration: the sounds the story itself names (doors, weather, crowds, machinery, water, footsteps, battle, bells — what the chapter is actually doing), placed at their moments, mixed UNDER the voices with the same ducking discipline. Effects never cover a spoken word, never startle over dialogue, and never run where the scene does not call for them.
3. **VERIFICATION** — no master enters the gate chain without a listen-check confirming both layers present and the balance measured (speech windows vs bed/effects windows), the same evidence form as the bed verifications of record.

OWNERS: Bard (audiobook line) with Dolby and the sound bench. IMMEDIATE APPLICATION: the belt-complete books in the finishing chain (Soldiers of Freedom first, then Kingdoms Before Chains) are built to this standard from Nov 25 2026; the already-finished set is AUDITED by title (bed present? effects present?) and any book missing either layer is named with its cure queued — nothing already shipped is silently left short of the standard. Every future audiobook is produced to this standard from its first mix block; the effects map is part of the production packet, not an afterthought.
