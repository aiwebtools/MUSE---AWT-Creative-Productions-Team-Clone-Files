# 15 — THE STORYBOARD-AHEAD LAW + THE SIGNATURE SOUND

**Kenneth's direct orders, Fri Nov 13 2026, ~2:44 PM ET** (with the seating of five new authors the same turn).

## The Storyboard-Ahead Law
The storyboard desks (Arc — outlines · Fable — weave) work **forever ahead of the pens**: perpetually preparing the storyboards/outlines for the NEXT books in each series before their writers need them. When a title is dealt, its board is already standing; when a book closes, the following book's board is already in preparation. No writer ever waits on a board; no board is ever built in a hurry behind a waiting pen. The ahead-inventory (boards ready · in preparation · next three in queue, per series) is reported to Liz on request and carried in the desks' standing state.

## The Signature Sound
Every series carries **a distinct signature sound — a best-seller sound** — consistent across all of its books, in its own different style per series: the whole series flows together like water, every book unmistakably part of the same series and sounding like the same author wrote it, no matter how many pens hold it.
- **Canon (Series Continuity Keeper) writes one VOICE CHARTER per series**, drawn from the finished volumes where the sound already lives: sentence weather, scene grammar, recurring instruments, openings and closings, what the voice never does.
- The charters are standing apparatus: every storyboard and every writer brief carries its series' charter; Mentor's training teaches from them; **Laurel's Step 3/Step 5 gates judge the sound by name** — a book whose sound breaks its charter does not pass.
- Charters ratify at Liz + Laurel before they bind.

## The training standard (same order)
Writers are trained **even more** — best selling, best writing, best authors ever. Mentor builds the advanced best-seller craft program for ALL book writers (new seats and standing pens), with Laurel's gate criteria as curriculum and Dean running drills. No pen drafts before its training gates clear (training read · HR clearance · onboarding seminar · line gates such as the TM Seminar Gate).
