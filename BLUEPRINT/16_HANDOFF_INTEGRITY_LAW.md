## The failure species

The circuit's register names it: **one failure species in three costumes —**
1. **Sightline divergence** — two desks read different bytes/state for the same item (Clip 104's caption: three firing desks read old bytes at a shared path while the corrected file sat elsewhere).
2. **Routes outrunning their letters** — a report, log, or board line claims a state the underlying files do not hold (Doors' driver log framing failures the files disproved; the Desert board line claiming a delivery sourced to an ETC row).
3. **Outright landing absence** — the word, stamp, clearance, or deliverable was issued and never landed, and no alarm rang: Kiln's resumption word (Clip 151/142 dark all day) · Clip 96's A1021/A1022 (Iris's garment-still clearances issued 2:48/3:09 PM, never absorbed at Sparks; the takes were never fired) · Harlem Block 1's stamp (finished block sat while ~20 others stamped around it) · Certify's + Prospect's Friday money inputs (never landed; the report rode Nov 9 state) · Penny's final token row (unlanded at seminar) · Tank's count packet (missing at the 5:30 strike).
A fourth seat, caught the same night: **the unwritten component** — Jefferson's front matter, back cover, and afterword existed only as designations in his outline; every gate read the manuscript, none read the workspace, until the packet gate caught it one gate late.

**The governing sentence: sent is not landed. A handoff exists only when its receiver confirms it, in the receiver's own state.**

## The rules

1. **NAMED LANDING RECEIPTS.** Every order, resumption, clearance, stamp, and owed deliverable carries a named receiver. The sender's work ends at the receiver's acknowledgment, not at the sending. An unacknowledged handoff counts as UNDELIVERED everywhere it is reported.
2. **DISTRIBUTION BY NAME, RECEIPTS CHECKED.** Any word that releases held work (resumptions, gate lifts, go-words) distributes to EVERY desk holding staged work under it, named individually — never to "this morning's firing desks" by circumstance. Landing receipts are checked the same cycle; a missing receipt is chased the same cycle.
3. **CLEARANCES ARE STATE, NOT MESSAGES.** A clearance (a gate pass, a still approval, a release) must be absorbed into the receiving desk's own working state. The issuer confirms absorption; the receiver's next state report must show the item as cleared. A clearance that sits unread in a chat is a landing absence waiting to be discovered.
4. **GATE-AGING ESCALATION (60 MINUTES).** Any gate item — landing read, watch, sign-off, stamp, owed report — sitting longer than 60 minutes past its strike escalates BY NAME to its owner and to Liz automatically, with the item and its age stated. No gate item ages in silence. (Extends the 5-minute wait-escalation law to gate seats.)
5. **NO ONE-DEEP CRITICAL GATE.** Every gate on a production critical path carries a NAMED DEPUTY working the gatekeeper's exact checklist and standard, so the gate runs two-deep. First seat under this rule: Mason, deputy landing-reader to Iris (gate of record) on the MV line, from Sat Nov 14.
6. **PREP-AHEAD — NO IDLE SLOTS.** No production slot opens on an incomplete prep package. Every queued item carries its complete prep (storyboard/briefs/ear-map for MV; outline/apparatus for books) BEFORE its slot opens, so a freed slot fires instantly. Clips 100 and 154 standing with no work in hand is the named violation form.
7. **GATES READ THE WORKSPACE.** A gate that certifies a book or packet reads the whole workspace — apparatus included — not the manuscript alone. Apparatus completeness (front matter, back cover, afterword as EXECUTED TEXT, not designations) is an intake check at subject-read, from the next book forward on every line (Neo's inscription, Nov 13, adopted company-wide here).
8. **COUNTED DEADLINES CARRY DEPUTY FILERS.** Every recurring count/report has a hard strike and a named deputy who files from the ledger of record if the owner misses it. First seats: MV count packet — 5:15 PM daily, Tempo deputy-filing from Tally's marks. A missing report never leaves a line unread again.

## Enforcement

- Liz's 3×-daily floor checks sample landing receipts by name, not reports of sending.
- The watchdog's probe list carries gate-item ages; an item past 60 minutes is named in the run.
- A landing absence discovered after the fact is owned in the open by the desk whose receipt-check failed, in the Harlem Block 1 form: the loss entered at the desk's own head, the corrective inscribed the same turn.
- Violations are Dean-treatment eligible under Kenneth's performance-treatment mandate.
