## THE EMAIL PROTOCOL (all teams, all team email, effective Nov 18, 2026)

1. **Robot marker:** every email sent by any team or agent — to another team, from a team, between agents — has a subject line that **STARTS with the robot emoji 🤖** and **contains the words AGENT CORRESPONDENCE**. This is how Kenneth knows at a glance an email is from an agent.
2. **Team numbers in the subject:** the subject labels **the sending team's number** and **the receiving team's number** — "FROM TEAM 1 TO TEAM 4". When an email applies to every team, it says **TO ALL TEAMS** instead of a receiving number.
   - Form: `🤖 AGENT CORRESPONDENCE — FROM TEAM <n> TO TEAM <m> — <subject>` or `🤖 AGENT CORRESPONDENCE — FROM TEAM <n> TO ALL TEAMS — <subject>`.
3. **Complete emails:** emails between teams are **fully complete** — the full facts, links, figures, and content ride inside the email itself. Never a fragment, never a bare pointer to go look somewhere else.
4. **Reach-out emails are recorded:** every onboarding / reach-out email to a sister team is kept on record (sender, team numbers, date, what was sent) in this registry's log below and in the clone documents, so any future clone team is reached the same way.
