## STEP 1 — THE PACKET (production side)

A finished book becomes a KDP packet before it is a listing. The packet stands in the KDP-ready home (Drive: "Sell This Shit - Muse" + the bundle "KDP Publishing - All Books - Muse") with everything together:

- The final book file per format, titled with ⭐ + COMPLETED + "UPLOAD THIS ONE" + its format qualifier.
- Copy-paste files labeled literally by purpose: BOOK DESCRIPTION · KEYWORDS · CATEGORIES · AUTHOR BIO (where a per-book bio stands) · SUBTITLE.
- "[Title] — FRONT COVER" and "[Title] — BACK COVER" images, each in BOTH JPEG and PNG.
- The folder titled "⭐ [Title] — COMPLETED — READY FOR SALE (KDP) — [date]".
- **The click test governs:** Kenneth opens the folder cold and knows what to click and what to paste.
- Local packet folders (~/workspace/kdp/<slug>/) are the production record and keep their working names; the click-test form governs the Drive homes.

## STEP 2 — THE COVER PROOF GATE (Parcel, inside Global's READY scan)

The cover hand of record (Parcel, where Parcel renders) proofreads the **exact shipped bytes** — front, back, and wrap — and the record rides IN the packet: reader's name + cover checksums + PASS or named defects. A re-render voids the proof; the proof re-runs on the new bytes. No packet files at Keymaker's desk and nothing scans READY without this record. (Full text: Blueprint 05, "The Cover Proof Gate," Kenneth's order Tue Nov 17, 2026.)

**THE FRONT-PARAGRAPH RULE (inscribed Thu Nov 19, 2026 — the Tubman Flag #1 lesson, ratified at Liz's chair): a descriptive paragraph on a FRONT cover is a DEFECT, whatever its wording.** A front carries the series line, the title in its governing form, the subtitle, the author, and the art — no prose paragraph, no reconstruction note, no blurb text. Such matter belongs in the front matter and on the back cover. A proof that reads a front paragraph as a designed element is struck as precedent (the Nov 17 Tubman proof so read it and passed it; Master Kenneth's live test rejected the class on sight, Nov 18–19, 2026). Any front carrying a paragraph is NOT READY; the cure drops the paragraph layer and nothing else, and the fresh proof runs on the cured bytes.

- **FRONT:** title exact (the full Title Form Law form entire on Resurrection titles — "Chasing ___: The AI Resurrection Diary of [Name]") · author name spelled right — **KG Bastian** on Kenneth's books, **V.C. Bastian** on Vanessa's · series branding correct · subject's likeness only, never Kenneth's face · zero typos on the face or the spine.
- **BACK:** synopsis proofread word for word off the rendered back cover (a shipping byte) · the 'A KG Bastian "AI Resurrection Diary" · aiwebtools.app' line on Resurrection titles · barcode/ISBN block present, correct, scannable · **no placeholder text, anywhere.**
- **WRAP:** spine exact · full-wrap dimensions, trim, and bleed to KDP spec per format (paperback + hardcover) · print-grade resolution.
- **FINISH:** ready-for-sale professional standard at thumbnail and at arm's length — hierarchy, alignment, contrast; no pixelation, no template artifacts.

# KDP REQUIREMENTS — EVERYTHING A TITLE MUST HAVE READY BEFORE ANYONE TOUCHES KDP
Built Wed Nov 18, 2026, from the live full-push — every item below is a lesson paid for today. A title that arrives without any item is NOT READY and does not get started. (Liz, Lead Agent — for all teams + the KDP desk.)

## 1. FILES — per title, all present and verified before setup begins
- KINDLE: manuscript EPUB (validated, opens clean, 0 spelling errors in the KDP preview) · Kindle FRONT cover (JPG/TIFF).
- PAPERBACK: interior PDF (print-ready) · full WRAP cover PDF (back + spine + front on ONE sheet, built to KDP spec for the book's exact trim size and page count).
- HARDCOVER: interior PDF · full WRAP cover PDF (hardcover wrap spec — it differs from paperback).
- Listing copy: the book description (written to sell), 3 categories, 7 keywords, series name + book number where it applies, author name exactly as it appears on the cover.

## 2. COVER LAW — the front/back rule (Kenneth's catch, Nov 18)
- A FRONT cover shows: the full title · the author name (KG Bastian / V.C. Bastian) · the cover art. It NEVER shows a barcode, a synopsis, or review quotes.
- A BACK cover shows: the synopsis · the series line · the barcode/ISBN space.
- A print WRAP is one sheet laid out BACK | SPINE | FRONT. A preview of a wrap can make the back panel look like the front — know the layout before you judge the file.
- BEFORE any upload, the cover hand opens the EXACT file being uploaded and verifies which cover it is. A wrap never goes in the Kindle cover slot. A back cover never goes in any front slot. A re-rendered cover voids the earlier verification — verify the shipped bytes, every time.
- Cover Proof PASS on the exact files (front, back, wrap, spine text, 300 DPI print resolution) is required before the title is called ready.

## 3. INTERIOR LAW
- MARGINS: every object on every page sits inside KDP's safe margins. (Paid for today: "When Loved Ones Leave Little Clues" hardcover — margin errors on pages 1, 3, 24, 25, 38, 67, 80 blocked approval.) Run the Print Previewer and clear EVERY flagged page before pricing.
- FONTS EMBEDDED in every print PDF.
- HARDCOVER needs a real book's page count (the 75+ page class); a too-short interior cannot ship as hardcover — say so early instead of discovering it at the previewer.
- Bleed/trim exactly per KDP spec for the format.

## 4. FIELDS — entered exactly, every title
- Title in its series form (Resurrection diaries: "Chasing ___: The AI Resurrection Diary of [Name]", series HISTORY RESURRECTED).
- Author: KG Bastian (Kenneth's books) · V.C. Bastian (Vanessa's books) — spelled exactly as on the cover.
- The book is NOT a public-domain work. Publishing territories = ALL territories (worldwide). (Either wrong setting caps the US royalty at 35%.)
- AI-CONTENT DISCLOSURE — Kenneth's standard form, verbatim selections: Texts: "Some sections, with minimal or no editing" · tool: ChatGPT. Images: "One or a few AI-generated images, with minimal or no editing" · tool: ChatGPT. Translations: None. The accuracy-confirmation box gets CHECKED. No other wording, no "entire work / extensive editing" form.

## 5. PRICING — for profit, checked per format before submit
- Standard ladder: chapter books + Time Machine $4.99 Kindle / $17.99 paperback / $29.99 hardcover · coloring $10.99 (Christmas Magic $8.99) · comics $14.99 · Little Lights $12.99 · Galactic Gambit Kindle $2.99.
- Every price passes a profit check against the format's print cost before submit. NEVER a placeholder or guessed price (paid for today: a $250 pair nobody would pay — repriced $19.99 / $29.99 by ruling).
- KINDLE ROYALTY: 70% requires the price inside $2.99–$9.99, all territories, not public domain. If the Amazon.com rate shows 35% on the pricing page: (1) re-enter the price fresh and let it recalculate; (2) if still 35%, set 9.99, Save as Draft, reopen, set the real price back; (3) if STILL 35% — STOP. Never publish at 35%. Hold the draft and report exactly what the page shows.

## 6. PROCESS — how the work runs
- One browser window, one page at a time. Never log out. Never delete anything.
- Name the title on the team thread BEFORE starting it — one team per title.
- Full look-over of every page of the setup before the submit click: details, content, covers, preview, pricing.
- Report the moment a title submits: title · format · price · resulting status (in review / publishing / live).
- Any error, block, or unexpected page: STOP, record exactly what the page shows, report it. Never guess past an error and never attest to anything you did not verify.
- A format sitting IN REVIEW cannot be edited — queue the change and fire it the moment the review clears.

## 7. AFTER KINDLE IS LIVE
- The Kindle edition must be LIVE before its audiobook can pair on ACX, and the ACX metadata must match the Kindle listing exactly (title form + author). Report the ASIN to Liz/Saga the turn it assigns.
