# 24 — THE QUOTA REMINDER LAW (Kenneth, Thu Nov 19, 2026)

Kenneth's standing law, set Nov 19, 2026: every production line carries a QUOTA REMINDER — a recurring reminder set at that line's real production time plus a buffer. It fires FOREVER, indefinitely, until Kenneth personally says stop. Its purpose, in his words: it keeps every author and every team on top of their game.

## The mechanism
When a reminder fires, the line lead roll-calls EVERY item on the line, by name, into exactly one of three states:
- (a) STILL BUILDING — item named, what remains stated (chapters / pages / stage).
- (b) FINISHED BUT NOT MOVED ON — a defect. The next item starts THAT TURN (outlines and next items are banked ahead precisely so this state is never excused by prep).
- (c) BLOCKED — the block, its owner, and its clock, named to Liz the same turn.
Lawful gates are states, named as such, never stalls: a Kenneth-hand decision (named Kenneth-hand, never chased), a quality gate with a named owner + running clock, a supply gate (e.g. coloring's parent confirmation), a provider limit in force.
An item that crosses TWO consecutive reminders without finishing escalates to Liz BY NAME with the reason.
