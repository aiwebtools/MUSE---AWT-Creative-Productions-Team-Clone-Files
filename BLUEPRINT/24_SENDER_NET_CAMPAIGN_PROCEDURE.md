## STEP 1 — THE PRE-WRITE SPEC (Campaign Builder discipline)

Every campaign is pre-written **end-to-end** by the Campaign Builder BEFORE any build in the account. The spec carries: subject line · preheader (preview text) · full copy · organization · button placement with each button's text + destination · its own color scheme · its image brief · promo code + price, every price verified against the live spa site. Specs live in SENDER_REBUILD_SPECS.md.

The build standard, every campaign, no exceptions:

- **Fully unique and completely new** — its own color scheme, its own wording, its own design. Near-identical campaigns are the defect Kenneth named on Nov 18; they do not ship.
- **Fresh generated images for every email**, generated ONLY through **Kenneth's ChatGPT account or his Gemini account** — no other generator.
- **Real customer-facing titles:** the campaign name IS the subject line. Internal codes, sequence numbers, and date scaffolding ("BFCM 2026 — 17 · Nov 26 · The Kickoff") are OFF every title, everywhere.
- **Subject line AND preview text** written correctly and set on every email.
- **Every button on the correct special's real buy link** — gift-card emails → the gift-card page; deal emails → the booking page.
- **The promo code in each email**, stated exactly as it redeems (see the open defect above).

## STEP 2 — THE BUILD

The Campaign Builder builds in Sender from the spec. The Spice hand's polish pass follows: color/design varied per email, layout finished, visual life in. The run board (BFCM_RUN_BOARD.md) tracks all 30 emails, #01–#30, two per day across the run.

## STEP 3 — THE TEST PROTOCOL (Kenneth, Nov 18, 2026)

Before any campaign is called finished:

1. The team sends **test emails TO EACH OTHER** and reviews them as a team.
2. The team **agrees the campaign is good** — clean and not sloppy, images right, reads exactly what it should read, real pricing and specials correct.
3. The **Screener click-tests every link in the test email** — every button, every linked image — through to the actual live buy pages, and confirms the destination is the right page for that special and the page is live and good to go.

A campaign that fails any part goes back to build. It does not advance on one person's say-so.

## STEP 4 — THE PROOF GATE (Kenneth's approval law)

- A **test email of every finished campaign goes to Kenneth's personal email — kgmasterbic@gmail.com — BEFORE any send**, so he can look at it himself and confirm the team is on track.
- **NOTHING sends to the list without Kenneth's approval of that individual email.** One approval covers one email. There is no blanket approval.
- The first campaign sends the moment its proof is approved — campaigns start **today**, the day the first proof clears, not on a later calendar date.
