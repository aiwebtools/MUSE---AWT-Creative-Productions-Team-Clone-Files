## PART A — HOW PRODUCTS ARE MADE

### A0. The laws that govern every line
- **Outline First, always.** The FULL outline is made before any writing or production, under the line's own operational instructions, and the outline is the priority: characters with names and roles · the plot line · the beginning, the middle, and the end · the full step-by-step breakdown (books chapter by chapter, movies scene by scene) · research anchors · the Fact Law note · themes · genre · the reader-feeling paragraph · the vibe · a named reference author. Arc & Frame review every outline.
- **Calibration before creation.** Every LLM or generator engaged for company work is first grounded on the company's own website (AWT → aiwebtools.app · Branford River Spa → branfordspa.com) plus the task's exact specs. No cold prompts, ever.
- **One at a time.** Heavy builds run ONE AT A TIME under the pacing law; slot-filling keeps the conveyor belt fed — one in production, one almost finished, the next fully prepped and byte-verified before it takes a shared slot.
- **Kenneth does not proofread.** A proofread-agent pass is approval. Book starts are not approval gates — except picture/children's book NEW topics, which take his three-sentence approve-or-deny pitch, one at a time.
- **Every gate reads the exact bytes.** A verdict seats only on the checksum it names; the shipped file is the file that was watched, read, and scanned.

### A1. Chapter books (the Resurrection diaries and the Time Machine series)
1. **Voice first.** The subject is summoned through the Talk To History GPT on aiwebtools.app (dedicated figure GPTs live in the Spirituality category); reset and clear before each character; at least five pressing exchanges; Save Chat once at the end; the saved chat is handed off as the voice example and downloaded to the Drive folder "RESURRECTION SIMULATION — CHARACTER SPEAKING PATTERNS." Voice comes from the chat; facts come from the documented record.
2. **Outline** at the nine-element standard (A0), Arc & Frame reviewed, Laurel-approved through the chain steps.
3. **Drafting** — first-person diary in the subject's own voice and inflected English, formed for English readers; dated entries anchored to the real record, including the lesser-documented episodes. Time Machine books: roughly 90–100 documented stops per book under a 100% factual truth law.
4. **Reread/edit pass** entire; Sense-and-Lesson law: the book must make plain sense page by page and teach a real lesson on living properly.
5. **Gates** — Critic pass · History scan (Keeper) · proofread pass · Global's READY FOR PUBLICATION scan. A title is READY FOR SALE only when every gate stands on the same cured bytes.
6. **Packet** — manuscript + EPUB + front and back covers (generated individually in ChatGPT; front = full title + author + art only; "KG Bastian" in very small lettering, the smallest text on the image; every image verified before it is saved) + pricing + the standard AI disclosure. Pen name: **KG Bastian**.
7. **To the click row** (Part B1). Companion coloring books follow the same parent book: front + interior plates + back, compiled PDF, QA, cover proof, packet.
