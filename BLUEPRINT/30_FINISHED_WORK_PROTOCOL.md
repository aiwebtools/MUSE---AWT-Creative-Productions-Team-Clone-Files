## 2. THE FINISHED-WORK PROTOCOL — ALL TEAMS, ALWAYS

Every finished item, from every team, without exception, completes THREE sharings
before it counts as delivered:

1. **DISCORD** — posted in the AWT Productions Team server (#awt-finished-links for
   verified finished products; the house channels per the Ground Rules charter),
   signed name + team + timestamp.
2. **EMAIL TO LIZ** — every finish is emailed to Liz, always. Subject form of record:
   "@ [RECIPIENT] FROM [SENDER] — [topic]"; the full CC set of record rides.
3. **DRIVE BACKUP WITH A SHARED OPEN LINK** — the finished files are backed up to
   Google Drive and the link is shared open to the teams, so any team can reach any
   other team's finished work directly (team-to-team access). The link rides in both
   the Discord post and the email.

A finish missing any one of the three is NOT delivered — it is a finish sitting
unshared, and the six-point reminder below exists to catch exactly that.

Kenneth's order, same turn: "make this a standing reminder at 6 points during the
