# 31 — SMOOTH OPERATIONS: THE FRICTION CURES

Kenneth's order, Tue Nov 24 2026, ~3:29 PM ET: "look for ways to make things go smoother between everyone and be more systemically organized and implement those."
Inscribed and implemented by Liz the same turn. Each law below answers a friction class PROVEN on the floor on Nov 24 2026 — the trigger case is named so the cure is never mistaken for decoration.

## 1. THE RULINGS REGISTER — one record of what governs
Trigger: Liz's 2:37 PM Nations email issued the opposite of her own 12:57 PM rulings because a stale state line still showed them "owed."
Law: every ruling enters `~/workspace/agents/RULINGS_REGISTER.md` the turn it is made. A desk acting on a ruled matter cites the register line. A superseded line is STRUCK in place, never deleted, and names its replacement. A ruling not in the register is reported to Liz, not acted on twice.

## 2. THE FILING-ROUTE LAW — a report is filed where its receiver works
Trigger: a writer's landing reports filed in her own board chat never reached the commissioning desk; finished chapters read as missing for a full cycle.
Law: every filing goes DIRECT to the desk that commissioned the work AND to Liz. A filing is not filed until the receiver confirms RECEIVED; silence past one hour = re-fire direct to the receiver by name. Filing into one's own board alone is a defect, named as such.

## 3. THE BOTH-RAILS COMPILE — no verdict from a partial read
Trigger: roll-call compiles read one rail and recorded misses against leads verifiably present on another; HR had to strike conversions.
Law: any compile that can mark a person missing, silent, or failed must read EVERY rail of record first (Full Team chat · Discord room · email). A rail that cannot be read marks the compile INCOMPLETE — an incomplete compile records NO misses, NO silences, NO failures. The verdict waits for the full read.

## 4. THE BYTE-CONFIRM HANDSHAKE — generalized
Trigger: the standing cross-desk visibility divergence (desks reading different bytes at one shared path, Nov 12 and again Nov 24).
Law: the inline-caption rule is now the general handoff rule — for ANY fire-critical file, the receiver reports the checksum it actually read before building on it. On mismatch, the sender's inline text/bytes govern and only that leg halts.

## 5. THE QUESTION QUEUE — no question parks
Trigger: a team lead's Nov 23 question batch still unanswered on Nov 24; seminar materials missing at the hour they were needed.
Law: every question asked of Liz enters `~/workspace/agents/QUESTIONS_OWED.md` the turn it is asked — asker · question · hour in · deadline the same production day. It leaves only when the answer is SENT and recorded. Liz reads the queue at every floor check; HR chases any line past deadline. Questions that are Kenneth's hand are marked as his in the queue — visible, never buried.

## 6. SESSION-ROUTE DISCIPLINE — jobs run where the session lives
Trigger: the room-keeping job attempted fresh logins on a dead route for hours while the live session stood open.
Law: every job that touches a signed-in surface names its ONE route of record in its body (device browser session, connector, CLI) and its stop-condition if that route is unavailable. A job never improvises a second route. (First application: the Discord room-keeping job, rewritten Nov 24 under the Device-Browser Law, Blueprint 30.)

## WHAT DID NOT CHANGE
Every gate, probe, checksum, and verification stands exactly as before. These laws govern how work TRAVELS between desks — never whether a check runs. Savings and smoothness live in the carrying, never in the checking.

## THE ALL-HANDS LAW — DOUBLE DECK, NEXT ONE READY (Kenneth's direct order, Tue Nov 24 2026, ~11:16 PM ET — "it is law")

Kenneth's words, in his order: get the whole crew in action · delegate tasks and future tasks, double deck — everything on deck and double deck · "that's how we do it, and that's how we will always do it, it is law" · ALWAYS HAVE THE NEXT ONE READY.

The law, operational:
1. ALL HANDS ON DECK: every production fires with its full crew mobilized — every seat named, tasked, and armed in parallel; no seat waits to be asked twice.
2. DOUBLE DECK: current tasks AND future tasks are delegated at the same time — the deck being worked and the deck staged behind it both carry named owners.
3. ALWAYS HAVE THE NEXT ONE READY: no item takes a slot until the item behind it is prep-complete and staged; the turn a slot frees, the next item fires with no idle gap. This restates and hardens the conveyor-belt and preparation laws as one standing mode, on every line — books, comics, picture books, coloring, music videos, movies, audiobooks.
4. The mode is permanent: it is not a push, a phase, or a campaign. It is how the house runs, always.
