1. **LIZ'S DAILY DIRECTIVE — EVERY TEAM, EVERY DAY, FRESH.** Each of Teams 2–7 receives its own email from Liz every morning: "@ TEAM N FROM LIZ — YOUR ORDERS: WHAT TO DO NEXT" — DO NEXT in working order with clocks · HOLD / DO NOT MOVE (gates, Kenneth's-hand items, blocked work with its blocker) · REPORT BACK (5:00 PM five-part status + the Finished-Work Protocol). Written for that day from the team's actual state; yesterday's text is never re-sent. Team 6's directive covers house items only (its lane rides its own lead channel).
2. **PART 2 — STILL TO BE COMPLETED.** Immediately after the directive, any team with formerly-assigned work still incomplete receives a second email: "@ TEAM N FROM LIZ — PART 2: STILL TO BE COMPLETED" — each outstanding item with its ORIGINAL assigned date and its current clock. An item leaves the list only when its completion verifies; being mentioned in a status does not close it. A team with nothing outstanding gets no Part 2, and the day's report says so by name.
3. **THE INDEPENDENCE LAW — THE KEY.** Every team's daily project set is standalone: completable by that team alone. A cross-team dependency inside a set is a defect, cured at generation (re-deal the item, or Team 1 supplies the input inside the package). Only when neither is possible does an item carry a named Team 1 fallback owner + clock, named in the package header and in that team's directive. No team's completion ever waits on another team's delivery.
4. **A BIT OF EVERYTHING.** Every team's daily set spans the project types (book · music video · comic · audiobook · picture book · coloring book · movie) in pairs of 2 per type per the Load Law, as supply allows — never a single-type day when other types have dealable stock. Shortages are named in the package header; out-of-load routes to Kenneth, never silent padding or off-spec invention.
5. **PREP AHEAD — THE FUTURE LIST.** ~/workspace/teams_packets/FUTURE_LIST.md: a rolling seven-day lookahead per team — upcoming items by name and type, blueprint link, outline state. Outlines for items three or more days out are started in prep capacity. Outline-first governs everything; the Daily Waves feed the book lines. No team ever opens a day unprepared.
6. **THE ASSIGNMENT LEDGER.** ~/workspace/teams_packets/ASSIGNMENT_LEDGER.md is the record of who has what assigned: item · assigned date · clock · state. Items enter the day they are assigned and leave only on verified completion; carried items keep their original assigned date (that date is what Part 2 prints). Kenneth's-hand items are listed as such and are never dealt to a team as completable work.
