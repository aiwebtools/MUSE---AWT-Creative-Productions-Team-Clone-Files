## PART 6 — THE DEFECT CLASSES, BY NAME (LEARN THESE ONCE, NEVER PAY FOR THEM AGAIN)

### Class A — Synthesis degeneration (audiobooks)
A text-to-speech voice, mid-sentence and at normal prosody, loops off the script into unscripted words, then recovers onto the script as if nothing happened. It sounds fluent. It is invisible to loudness checks, spot samples, and waveform reads. The only instrument that sees it is a full transcription of the actual audio read against the script, line by line. A genuine case reproduces the same wrong words identically across two different transcription engines — that double agreement is the conviction standard.

### Class B — Synthesis improvisation (audiobooks) — named by Liz's ruling, Nov 24 2026
A substituted or invented clause inside an otherwise correct sentence: head and tail intact, duration normal, nothing loops. Only an exact stem-level read against the script catches it. One confirmed seat supersedes that chapter's earlier clean verdict on that sentence, and its clearance is re-earned after the cure, never assumed.

The cure for both classes: re-synthesize at the smallest width that holds the defect — the sentence, split-fed if the turn is long — then re-fold, then the full gate chain re-runs on the new checksum. Never re-record a whole chapter for one sentence; never ship a chapter on a sample.

### Class C — The conversion re-form (video) — the night's recurring defect, Nov 24 2026
Direct still-to-video conversion quietly redraws what it was given: a cloth coat returns as glossy leather, the Light Within settles into a printed graphic on a shirt, light code resolves into legible letters or musical notes, bass strings thin to guitar weight, ears grow unboarded jewelry, hair drifts. The motion is usually true while the forms are false — which is why motion checks alone pass defective takes. The road, in order: (1) the Form Lock rides at headline on every fire — wardrobe, chest, ears, instruments, and code named as holds in their plate forms at every frame; (2) when the lock fails on an axis, the seat routes to CONSTRUCTION: a brief that keeps the landed take's certified performance entire and rebuilds only the re-formed zones on the take's own bytes, the hardest axis gated separately so a miss returns alone and no partial build ever ships; (3) the fallback is priced in advance, at the moment the direct fire is priced, so no seat ever waits on a fresh pricing round.

### Class D — Cover and packet form defects
The printed back cover carries the packet's back-cover text ENTIRE — preview sections included; nothing is "listing only" unless the text of record says so. The front carries the series line. The EPUB embeds the composed front cover, byte-equal. Wraps are proofed as sheets — paperback and hardcover separately — faces full, barcode seat clear, every line read seat by seat against the text of record. A cover proof signed without that read is not a proof; this house paid for that lesson on Nov 24 and it is not for sale again.

## PART 8 — TOKEN ECONOMY, STORAGE, AND SECURITY (THE INVISIBLE DISCIPLINES)

Token economy — the cost of this operation is thread length times message count; output is a rounding error. Therefore:
- Context lives in a STATUS file, one per production, compact and current. A fresh thread per item, seeded from the status packet. Never carry a production in conversation memory across days.
- One message per completed step, never per probe. Delta-only filings: a settled record is never re-filed in full.
- Probe, do not pour: counts, tails, single values. Whole logs, whole transcripts, and whole settled records never pour into a thread.
- A lead owns every token its delegated agents burn. The largest single leak this house ever found was invisible until delegated runs were rolled up to their parent desks — a third of a day's entire burn. Delegate with a budget in mind: small, well-briefed, checkpointed runs beat one giant wandering run.
- Savings live in how results are carried, never in whether the check runs. Gates, probes, byte checks, and verification reads are never cut.

Storage:
- Drive is home for everything. Local disk runs under a ceiling (83 percent working, 85 locks); finished folders are offloaded to Drive after byte-and-checksum verification, and nothing local is deleted before its Drive copy verifies.
- Save as you go: every working file goes to Drive in the same motion it is made — drafts, chapters, plates, stills, takes, packets, records.
- Backups on trouble: when crashes or restarts hit an active batch, snapshot every in-flight project folder first and push the resumability layer (transcripts, storyboards, scripts, audits, logs) to the category Drive folder — before relaunching anything.
- Superseded versions move to a superseded folder inside the packet. Never deleted, never shipped. The file of record is named in the status note, and when two versions compete, Liz judges; no desk ships the newer one by default.

Security:
- Phishing is constant. A billing scare email with an order number and a phone number to call is a scam until proven otherwise — never call its numbers, never click its links; report it to Liz. (Worked example, Nov 24: a fake Geek Squad charge for 291.93 dollars urging a call to a number that is not Best Buy's.)
- Login alerts, passkey notices, and new-device warnings on any house account are reported to Liz the same hour they land. Two-factor authentication stays enabled. House account credentials are never shared outside the team's own seats and never written into a document, a chat, or an email body.
- When a platform changes under you — a new checkbox, a moved button, a step that looks like compliance — record it exactly, report the fact, and never improvise around it. (Worked example, Nov 24: KDP's current Content page carries no AI-accuracy checkbox; the correct move was to verify its absence on two titles and strike it from the checklist, not to hunt for a way to check it.)

## PART 11 — EDITING STRATEGIES (HOW THIS HOUSE FIXES THINGS)

1. EAR MAP BEFORE BOARD. A music video or film scene is planned from a timed map of the actual audio — every lyric, entrance, dip, and climax timed to the second — and the board is built on the lyric itself, not on a vibe. A board built without the ear map is rebuilt.
2. KEEP THE PERFORMANCE, CURE THE FORM. When a take's performance is certified and its forms are wrong, never throw the take away and never re-roll the whole thing: build on its bytes in tracked zones (construction), one zone per defect axis, the hardest axis gated separately. A miss on one zone returns that zone alone.
3. CURE AT THE SMALLEST WIDTH THAT HOLDS THE DEFECT. One wrong sentence in an audiobook chapter is re-synthesized at sentence width (split-fed when the turn is long), re-folded, and re-gated on the new checksum — the chapter is never re-recorded entire, and the cure is never waved through on a sample.
4. TWO RECOGNIZERS FOR A CONVICTION. A transcription disagreement is settled by a second engine: identical wrong words on both is a defect of record; a split read is a characterization task, not a shipment and not a panic.
5. THE REREAD/EDIT PASS IS A PRODUCTION STEP, NOT A COURTESY. Every manuscript gets a full reread and edit pass after drafting and before compile: sense page by page, the lesson landing, voice consistency, the fact law. Then the proof chain runs on the shipping bytes.
6. SEAT-BY-SEAT COVER PROOF. The compositor's eye goes line by line against the text of record — title, subtitle, series line, the full back text with its preview, the author line smallest on the image — on the actual wrap sheet, both formats. Findings are named by seat, cures are verified by re-read, not by report.
7. TWO TAKES MINIMUM, A NAMED PICK. Generation seats produce at least two takes of anything that matters; a named director's eye picks; the pick and its reason are recorded. First-output acceptance is how defects ship.
8. SKIP-IF-EXISTS, CHECKPOINT BEFORE EXPENSIVE. Renders and builds skip what already exists and is verified. Before any expensive phase, the status note, transcript, storyboard, and scripts are on disk, so a dead run resumes from files, never from scratch.
9. PLATE-CHAPTER MATCH (coloring and picture lines). Every plate must depict its own chapter's scene. A beautiful plate of the wrong scene is a defect, full stop, and it is caught at the key-filing stage — the written key for every plate is filed before generation, so a mismatch is visible before a single image is made.
10. THE CLOSING-SPAN CHECK. Video gates read the whole file, with named attention to the final span — bookends, closing frames, and the last seconds of audio are where unexamined defects live. A gate that stops early is a gate that lies.
11. NAME THE CLASS, NOT JUST THE INSTANCE. Every cured defect gets its class named and its check added to the line's gate list the same day. Fixing an instance without fixing the class is renting the same defect again next week.

## PART 12 — AUDIOBOOKS: WHY MP3 AND MP4 ARE DIFFERENT, AND WHAT EACH ONE INCLUDES

An audiobook leaves this house in two different packages, because its two homes want two different things. Confusing them is a beginner error this packet ends tonight.

THE MP3 PACKAGE — THE AUDIO PRODUCT ITSELF.
- What it is: pure audio. One MP3 file per chapter, plus an opening credits file, a closing credits file, and a retail sample file. No picture of any kind.
- Where it goes: the audiobook storefronts (ACX and its channels) — the product a listener buys, downloads, and plays in an audio app.
- What it includes, every time: the full-cast or narrated performance exactly as gated · the spoken disclaimer at the head of the book · chapter files as SEPARATE files (a single merged file fails the storefront's intake) · loudness and bed within spec on every file · the three gate signatures earned on the same checksum the files carry (Part 13).
- The rule of identity: the MP3 files are the master audio. Nothing is ever re-recorded, re-mixed, or "improved" for another package without the whole gate chain re-running on the new bytes.

THE MP4 PACKAGE — THE VIDEO PRESENTATION OF THE SAME BOOK.
- What it is: a video file — the identical gated master audio married to visuals: the book's cover presentation and page-turn visuals, so the book can live on video platforms that cannot host a bare audio file.
- Where it goes: ONLY YouTube, the AWT Audiobooks Facebook Page, and the Finished Audiobooks shelf. Nowhere else, ever.
- What it includes, every time: the same master audio, byte-identical in substance to the MP3 package · the spoken disclaimer audible at the head · the cover and page-turn visual track for the full duration · captions where the line's ruling seats them · the same three gate signatures, on the same checksum, earned before any post.
- Why it exists at all: discovery. A listener finds the book as a video, hears the performance, and goes to the storefront for the audio product. The MP4 is the shop window; the MP3 is the goods.

THE DIFFERENCE IN ONE LINE: the MP3 is the book as a product you own; the MP4 is the book as a performance you can watch being found. Same voice, same gates, same checksum discipline — two packages, two homes, never substituted for each other. An MP3 posted where an MP4 belongs will not play; an MP4 delivered where an MP3 belongs will be rejected at intake. Check the destination before you build the package.

## PART 14 — HOW TO BE A SUCCESSFUL TEAM LEAD: DELEGATION, AND WHEN TO ADD AGENTS

A lead's job is not to do the most work. A lead's job is to make the whole team finish verified product every day, and more of it than yesterday.

THE CRAFT OF IT:
- Know every desk's live item and clock at all times. If you cannot say what each of your people is doing right now, you are not leading this hour; go find out.
- Delegate by lane and by strength: the desk that gates well gates; the desk that writes fast drafts; nobody is assigned by rotation or by fairness — by fit, with the reason nameable.
- Every assignment carries four things: the item by its full plain title, the inputs verified in hand, the done definition, and the clock. An assignment missing any of the four is a misunderstanding scheduled for later.
- The handoff is not done when you send it. It is done when the receiver confirms, with the checksum they read. Chase silence; silence is never receipt.
- Carry a NEXT list for every desk (the next items in order, at least twenty deep on the team board). A desk that finishes pulls the next item the same turn. A desk with no written next item is a desk about to idle.
- Check constantly, kindly, and by name: midday is too late to discover a morning stall. Ping early, help fast, praise publicly, correct privately and precisely.
- Protect the gates. Never let a deadline talk you into shipping around a gate seat. A defective finish counts zero and costs the rework besides; the gate is the cheapest moment to be right.
- Report in three lines: doing · completed · next. Verdicts and decisive numbers; the long form lives in the packet.
- Own your tokens like money: brief your agents small and precisely, checkpoint their work to files, and read their status notes instead of their whole threads.

WHEN TO ADD AGENTS (staff to the need — the standing authorization):
- A queue deeper than three items at one hand gains a named second hand the same cycle.
- A line heading for zero on the day gains hands at the moment the zero is visible, not at the count.
- The same gap named twice becomes a seat: if the team has twice lacked a checker, a builder, a poster — hire the seat.
- A new line or a new series expansion gets its desks at dealing time, not after the backlog forms.
- Every added agent gets, before its first production shift: a name and a lane, a written instruction file (lane · sources of truth · hard rules · cadence · current state), its own workstation, and a verification that it has read its instructions and its lane's standards. An agent without those is a rumor, not a desk.
- And the reverse discipline: an agent with no queue is reassigned the same day. Headcount follows work, never habit.
