## THE METHOD
1. **Claim first.** Read ~/workspace/college_degrees/DEGREE_CLAIMS.md; claim the degree before starting (degree · institution · agent · date). No duplicates; the two generator agents coordinate through the ledger.
2. **Top three schools.** Research the top three institutions in existence for the degree; pick the most fleshed-out published program.
3. **Emulate the institution.** The book mirrors that school's curriculum structure, course sequence, and teaching methods; the teaching voice sounds like a professor from that school.
4. **Teach every course in full.** Syllabus · lessons taught in full · readings · worked examples · images/diagrams/charts · assignments · assessments with keys/rubrics · capstone. Compile course-by-course into the degree's single DOCX/PDF; STATUS.md tracks taught vs. the curriculum map.
5. **Brand it.** College Degree GPT + aiwebtools.app + Eye of Providence logo: cover, running headers, certificate.
6. **Close the degree.** Final page = the spoof degree certificate (plainly a spoof). Compile the final DOCX + PDF; verify (page count, cover, branding, certificate present, no placeholder text).
7. **Bank it.** Drive: COLLEGE DEGREE CLASSES / [Institution] — [Degree Title] / finals. Local finals stay in the degree folder.
8. **Distribute it.** (a) The daily Lovable run adds it to the College Degree GPT app's free downloadable lineup and verifies it appears. (b) A KDP packet is prepared to the click, priced for profit, standard AI disclosure; print page ceilings may split a degree into volumes — name the split in the packet.
9. **Deal the next.** The generator claims the next unclaimed degree from the queue the act one banks (conveyor law).
