## CONVEYOR BELT DOCTRINE (Kenneth, Nov 1 2026)

Production runs like a **conveyor belt**: the moment a job finishes and capacity
frees up, the next queued item starts immediately. Fill every gap, keep the belt
fed, work continuously for as long as the slate has items — no idle capacity, ever.
The belt is fed by **slot-filling inside the one-at-a-time pacing law** (Oct 31
2026): one heavy build at a time, and the next one fires the second its slot frees —
never a stampede of parallel launches. When one production line runs dry, pull the
next item from the standing slate order (real versions → movie scenes → parked
queue prep) rather than stopping.

## THE THREE JOBS OF THIS LOG (Kenneth, Nov 1 2026)

This log exists so Muse gets smarter and never f**** himself by leaving a
gap that a future production falls through. It does three jobs at once:

that is the mechanism. An entry that turns out wrong gets corrected in place,
never left standing next to its correction.
